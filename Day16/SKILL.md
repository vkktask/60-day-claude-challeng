# stock-fundamental-research

Skill to analyze Indian and global listed stocks using fundamentals only. Evidence-based reports across 5 modes. Never gives buy/sell/hold recommendations.

## Modes
- Quick Take (default) - single stock, short analysis
- Deep Dive - full 8-tab HTML report with charts
- Compare - two stocks side by side
- Pros & Cons - strengths and risks
- Portfolio Fit - how a stock fits your holdings

## Mandatory Rules
1. Live data first: Screener > Tickertape > Moneycontrol > NSE > BSE > Annual Reports
2. Never fabricate data. Flag unavailable data with source
3. Cite source beside every key figure
4. No buy/sell/hold calls, target prices, or investment advice
5. No predictions
6. Plain English, explain jargon
7. Always include price chart

## Research Checklist
CMP, Market Cap, Face Value, 52W High/Low
P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA vs sector and 5Y avg
Revenue, Profit, EPS CAGR (3Y/5Y)
EBITDA Margin and NPM (5Y trend)
EPS last 8 quarters
FCF (3-5Y)
D/E, Interest Coverage, Current Ratio
ROE and ROCE (current, 3Y avg, 5Y avg)
Dividend history and payout
Promoter holding trend and pledging (flag if >10%)
FII/DII trends (8 quarters)
Moat, pricing power, brand, switching costs, market share
Management quality and governance
Sector tailwinds/headwinds
Latest earnings commentary
Top news
3 closest peers: P/E, P/B, ROE, Revenue Growth, D/E

## Interpretation Rules
Valuation: Cheap = below sector and history; Fair = within 10%; Expensive = above both
D/E: <1 Safe, 1-2 Moderate, >2 Leveraged
Interest Coverage: >3 Healthy, 1.5-3 Watch, <1.5 Risk
Current Ratio: >1.5 Comfortable, 1-1.5 Watch, <1 Risk
FCF: Positive and growing = Strong; Stable = Stable; Negative = Concern
ROE/ROCE: >15 Good, 10-15 Average, <10 Weak
Growth: Accelerating / Steady / Slowing / Declining

## Output: Deep Dive
8-tab dark-theme HTML report with Chart.js charts.
Tabs: Snapshot, Valuation, Growth, Health, Returns, Peers, Ownership, View
View tab: strengths, watch-points, key metric, quality rating, data confidence, disclaimer

## Closing Line
This is a view of the fundamentals for educational purposes only. It is not investment advice and not a buy/sell/hold recommendation. Verify all figures independently. The final decision is yours.
