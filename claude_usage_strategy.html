Biggest Mistakes to Avoid
⚡

Using Max Effort for Everything

Max effort is slow and costs more. Using it for bullet edits or quick questions wastes your time. Reserve it for critical, high-stakes outputs only.

🤏

Using Haiku for Complex Strategy

Haiku will give shallow answers to deep questions. Never use it for business plans, career decisions, or complex research — you'll get mediocre output.

📋

Vague Prompts

"Help me with my resume" gives average results. "Rewrite my resume for a Senior Product Manager role at a Series B SaaS startup, emphasizing metrics" gets exceptional results.

🔁

Not Iterating

Don't accept the first output. Use Claude as a collaborator: "Make this more concise," "Add a stronger hook," "What am I missing?" Iteration is where the magic happens.

🧩

Asking Everything in One Prompt

Don't dump 5 tasks into one message. Break complex work into focused sessions: research → outline → draft → refine. Each step gets better output.

📎

Not Giving Context

Tell Claude who you are, what the document is for, and what tone you want. "I'm a software engineer pivoting to product management, targeting mid-size tech companies" changes everything.
